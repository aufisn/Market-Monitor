Watchlist as a card stack (steps 1 and 4)

Mock-up with made-up prices. Swipe or drag the card, or tap a symbol.

Watchlist

It stops at the first and last card (no wrap-around).

  • Each card has its own Alerts switch. Turning one stock off doesn't affect the others.
  • "Disable all alerts" turns every card's switch off. To turn alerts back on, use the switch on each card.
  • A stock with alerts off has its symbol struck through in the strip, and its card is dimmed.
  • Opens on the most urgent stock. The order then stays fixed while you use it.
  • A red dot on a symbol marks an active alert. Alert cards have a red border.
  • Only a sideways drag changes the card, so scrolling up and down still works.
