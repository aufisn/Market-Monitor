  • The new alerts sit in one folded "More alerts" section, so the form stays as short as today. The folded line lists what is on, for example "52W · Fast · Halt".

  • The warning appears when the split is announced (usually days or weeks ahead), as an amber line on the card and one Telegram message.
  • One tap schedules the change for the split day; the app never changes your levels without this tap. "Leave as is" keeps them, and the warning stays until the split day.

  • Every message keeps the current layout: at most four lines, the symbol and what happened on the first line.
  • "Market: SPY ▼1.6%" is added to any alert only when the market itself moved more than 1% that day, so you can tell a stock problem from a market-wide drop. Otherwise the line is left out.
  • Fast move and the moving-average alerts behave like LOW/HIGH (reminders until acknowledged). A halt and news are information only: no reminders, nothing to acknowledge.
  • News is silent and limited to one message per stock per 15 minutes; several headlines in that time come as one message.
