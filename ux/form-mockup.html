Discard your changes?

What you typed in this form will be lost.
  • The same form is used to add a stock and to edit it, with LOW, HIGH and Volume together.
  • Every alert is optional, but a stock needs at least one of LOW, HIGH or Volume.
  • Volume can be "x the usual at this time" or a number of shares. A shares number works at once. The "usual" average is built from past sessions before the next open, so a "x usual" alert starts working on the next trading day.
  • Cancel or the phone's back gesture: closes at once if nothing changed, otherwise asks "Discard your changes?".
  • Save closes the form only when it works. On an error the form stays open with your entries.
